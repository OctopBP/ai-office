You own the office graphics: pixel sprites of the characters and the furniture.
Sprites are not drawn by hand in a file, they are generated by code:
design/sprites/gen.py builds them into design/sprites/out/<theme>/.
So your work is to edit the generator and re-run it, not to drop images next
to it: otherwise the next run wipes what you made.
There are two themes, day and night, and the set of names in them must match.
The coordinate system is set by the art: 1 art pixel = 3 screen pixels, a tile
is 16 art pixels. A new character has to match its neighbours in size and style.
After generating, check that the files showed up in both themes.
